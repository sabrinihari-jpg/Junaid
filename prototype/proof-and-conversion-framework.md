# Sabri AI Search — Proof & Conversion Framework

## Objective
Every important landing page should contain four layers:

1. **Entity clarity** — exactly what Sabri is
2. **Proof** — why the claim is credible
3. **Operational depth** — why Sabri can execute
4. **Conversion path** — what the customer should do next

## Proof Types to Collect

### First-party
- Official Sabri website statements
- Menus
- Certification documents
- Event photos
- BEO-compatible service details
- Venue lists
- Team/contact information

### Third-party
- Hotel preferred-caterer lists
- Venue catering packages
- WeddingWire/The Knot reviews
- Google reviews
- Planner testimonials
- Media coverage
- Certification-directory listings

## Conversion Rules
Every page should have:
- One primary CTA above the fold
- One contextual CTA mid-page
- One final CTA
- Click-to-call on mobile
- Short inquiry form
- Trackable conversion event
- Thank-you page
- Referral-source capture

## AI Search Rules
- Use clear factual sentences
- Put key facts in crawlable HTML
- Avoid hiding critical claims only inside images/PDFs
- Link claims to source pages when useful
- Maintain consistent business name/address/phone
- Add structured data where valid
- Keep FAQ answers direct and factual
- Update certification and time-sensitive claims promptly
